Metro Downtown questions

Frequently asked questions from downtown clients

Do you charge extra to work in downtown high-rises? expand_more
No surcharge for high-rise work itself. However, buildings that require a COI (Certificate of Insurance) on file, freight elevator reservations, or after-hours access may incur a small coordination fee. We'll always tell you upfront before we schedule the job.
Can you work around our restaurant's operating hours? expand_more
Yes. Most of our Restaurant Row work happens between 10pm and 6am, or during scheduled closing days. We coordinate directly with your manager and health department requirements so service never interrupts dining.
Do you handle commercial grease trap compliance for the health department? expand_more
We do. Every pump-out includes a signed manifest, before/after photos, and documentation formatted for health department inspections. We can also set up recurring scheduled service so you're never caught off-guard.
How quickly can you reach a downtown emergency? expand_more
We keep dedicated crews staged in the CBD and Financial District during business hours, and on-call overnight. Average emergency response time in Metro Downtown is 28 minutes — faster than our tri-county average because of the density.
Can you provide a Certificate of Insurance for our building? expand_more
Absolutely. We carry $2M general liability and workers' comp. Send your building's COI requirements to service@waltsplumbing.com and we'll have it back to your property manager within one business day.
Do you work with body corporates and property managers on retainer? expand_more
Yes. We offer preferred-rate service agreements for downtown property managers and body corporates. This includes priority dispatch, consolidated monthly invoicing, and a dedicated account contact. Call us to discuss a custom plan.
