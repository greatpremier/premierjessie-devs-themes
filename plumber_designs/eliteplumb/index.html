Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding our dispatch speed, price guarantees, and licensing standards.

How fast can an emergency technician arrive at my home or business? expand_more

For priority emergencies (active burst lines, sewer back-ups, gas leaks), our mobile vans are dispatched immediately with an average arrival window of 30 to 45 minutes across the metropolitan service area. Our live operators communicate GPS tracking updates in real time.

Do you provide upfront quotes before starting the repair? expand_more

Yes. We operate on a strict transparent flat-rate pricing schedule. Following on-site inspection, our licensed technician presents an itemized quote detailing labor and parts before any tools touch your pipes. You will never encounter hidden surcharges or unexpected line items.

Are your technicians fully licensed, bonded, and insured? expand_more

Every field technician at ElitePlumb holds active state Journeyman or Master Plumbing credentials, is fully background-checked, drug-tested, and covered by our comprehensive $2,000,000 general liability policy and worker's compensation.

What warranties or guarantees come with your repairs? expand_more

All standard plumbing repairs come backed by our 1-year craft warranty. Equipment replacements (including tankless water heaters and whole-home filtration) carry full manufacturer warranties extending up to 10 to 12 years, coupled with our 2-year complimentary labor guarantee.

Do you work after hours and on weekends? expand_more

Yes. We run emergency callouts 24/7, including weekends and public holidays. After-hours rates apply and are always quoted upfront before we dispatch anyone, so there are no surprises.

Do you handle insurance claims? expand_more

We do. We provide detailed reports, photos and itemised invoices that insurers accept, and we are happy to deal with your assessor directly to speed things up.
