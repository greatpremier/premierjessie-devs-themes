Downtown Specialists

Plumbing Downtown isn't like plumbing anywhere else

Downtown Metropolitan City has some of the oldest residential plumbing stock in the metro — turn-of-the-century cast iron drains, original galvanised supply lines, and shared risers that require careful pressure management. Add high-rise apartment towers, mixed-use buildings, and strict building management rules, and you need a plumber who has worked these systems hundreds of times.

ElitePlumb has been serving Downtown properties for over 18 years. We know which buildings have known riser issues, which property managers require COI certificates before arrival, and how to work quietly and cleanly in occupied apartments.

Downtown FAQ

Questions from Downtown clients

Specific to the buildings, codes, and quirks of the district.

How fast can you get to a Downtown address? expand_more

Our Downtown average arrival time is 42 minutes for emergency calls. We keep two vans stationed in the district during business hours and one on-call overnight, so we're rarely more than 15 minutes from any Downtown address.

Do you carry the insurance certificates Downtown buildings require? expand_more

Yes. We maintain a $2,000,000 general liability policy and can email a Certificate of Insurance (COI) naming your building or management company as additional insured, usually within 30 minutes of a booking. We're already on file with most major Downtown property managers.

Can you work in a pre-war building without damaging historic plaster or tile? expand_more

Absolutely — it's a specialty. We use acoustic leak detection and thermal imaging to locate problems without opening walls unnecessarily. When access is required, we cut clean, small openings and coordinate with plasterers and tilers on our trusted referral list.

Do you handle riser and stack work in high-rise buildings? expand_more

Yes. We routinely work on vertical risers and soil stacks in high-rise residential and commercial buildings. We follow building management's notification requirements for neighbours above and below, and we schedule shut-offs during agreed low-usage windows.

What's the most common plumbing problem you see Downtown? expand_more

Three things: (1) root intrusion into original clay sewer laterals on tree-lined blocks, (2) scale and hard-water damage to water heaters and fixtures, and (3) failed shut-off valves in pre-war apartments. We address all three routinely and can advise on prevention.

Are after-hours rates higher for Downtown calls? expand_more

After-hours and weekend rates apply, and are always quoted upfront before we dispatch a van. Downtown dispatch is available 24/7 including public holidays — no exceptions.
