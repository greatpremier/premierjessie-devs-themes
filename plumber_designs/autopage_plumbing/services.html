Protecting Your Home from Municipal Pressure Surges & Water Hammer

Due to persistent reservoir load-shifting across Sandton, Randburg, and Midrand, municipal mains frequently experience catastrophic pressure spikes up to 900+ kPa when lines recharge. This sudden kinetic shock shatters geyser cylinders, blows out flexible connector hoses, and ruptures internal joints.

Autopage Plumbing installs upstream brass Pressure Reducing Valves (PRVs) with inline pressure gauges at your water meter, stabilizing inlet supply to a safe, constant 400 kPa, protecting your entire property.

400 kPa Calibrated Safe Operating Target
PRV Model: Apex / Caleffi Master PRV
