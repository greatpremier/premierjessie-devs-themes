Immediate Action Protocol

Follow these 4 safety containment actions right now while our technician is en route to prevent structural damage.

Call Tech For Step-By-Step Guidance
01
faucet

Step 1: Shut Main Municipal Meter

Locate your Johannesburg Water boundary meter (usually situated in the pavement box or front garden boundary). Turn the brass wheel cock clockwise 90 to 180 degrees until fully tightened to halt municipal pressure.

check_circle Prevents internal structural flooding
02
electric_bolt

Step 2: Kill DB Geyser Breaker

Head to your main Distribution Board (DB). Locate the breaker toggle clearly labeled "Geyser" or "Water Heater". Flip it downward into the OFF position immediately to protect the heating element and prevent an electrical fire.

check_circle Stops element burnout & electrical shorts
03
water_drop

Step 3: Open Lowest Garden Tap

Walk to your lowest outdoor garden bibcock or driveway spigot and turn it on full. This evacuates head pressure trapped within internal copper/PEX lines, drawing standing water away from your ceilings and cabinetry.

check_circle Drains line water outside the structure
04
photo_camera

Step 4: Photograph All Damage

Take wide and close-up digital photos and videos of flooded flooring, saturated ceiling drywall, affected furniture, and the burst origin. Do not discard ruptured fittings — our technician attaches them directly to your claim docket.

check_circle Expedites 100% insurance reimbursement
