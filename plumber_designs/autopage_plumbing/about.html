Engineering Excellence, Uncompromising Integrity.

Founded in Sandton in 2008, Autopage Plumbing was established on a radical principle: to replace the reckless guesswork, opaque pricing, and unaccountable craftsmanship of legacy operators with systematic civil-grade plumbing engineering across Greater Johannesburg.

From emergency high-pressure burst main remediation in Rosebank corporate towers to bespoke solar geyser retrofits in Bryanston estates, our PIRB-registered master technicians operate with rigorous technical compliance to SANS 10252-1 standards. We treat residential and commercial infrastructure not as routine pipes, but as vital life-support hydraulics.
