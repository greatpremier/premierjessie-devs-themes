Questions

Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Call us — we'll give you a straight answer.

Do you charge a callout fee?
We charge a standard callout fee that covers travel and the first assessment. If you accept the quote, that fee is credited against the final invoice. You'll always know the fee before we arrive — no surprises.
How quickly can you get to me?
For emergencies — burst pipes, flooding, no water — we aim for same-day response across Johannesburg, usually within 2 to 4 hours. For standard bookings we can typically be there within 24 to 48 hours. Sandton, Randburg, Midrand and Roodepoort are our fastest areas.
Do you work after hours and on weekends?
Yes. We run emergency callouts on weekends and after hours. After-hours rates apply and are quoted upfront before we dispatch anyone.
Will I get a written quote and invoice?
Always. You receive a written quote before work starts and a tax invoice when it's done. Body corporates, landlords and insurers can be invoiced directly — just give us the billing details.
Are you registered and insured?
Yes. We're a registered business with a company registration number and VAT number, we carry public liability insurance, and our compliance details are listed in the footer of this site. We're happy to send our documentation on request.
Is your work guaranteed?
All workmanship is guaranteed in writing. Manufacturer warranties on fittings and geysers are passed on to you with the paperwork. If something we installed fails within the guarantee period, we come back and fix it.
Do you handle insurance claims?
We do. We provide detailed reports, photos and itemised invoices that insurers accept, and we're happy to deal with your assessor directly to speed things up.
