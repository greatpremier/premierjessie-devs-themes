What Northfield homeowners ask us most

How fast can you actually reach Northfield? expand_more
Our Northfield crews are staged from a hub on the Highlands junction, so average emergency arrival is 38 minutes. For a burst pipe or flooding situation we prioritise you immediately and, if you call from a Northfield landline or mobile within the suburb, we'll often be there in under 30. For non-emergency bookings we typically schedule within 24–48 hours.
Do you charge extra for weekend or after-hours callouts in Northfield? expand_more
No. Northfield emergency rates are the same flat rate as weekday daytime calls, including weekends and public holidays. You'll know the exact figure over the phone before we dispatch. This is a Northfield policy — we live and work here too.
Can you handle heritage homes on Grand Avenue and Old Mill District? expand_more
Absolutely — heritage plumbing is one of our Northfield specialities. We're familiar with the original galvanised and cast-iron systems common in Grand Avenue cottages and the stacked drainage of converted Old Mill lofts. We take extra care not to disturb original plaster, cabinetry, or facade details, and we can source period-correct fittings where restoration is the goal.
Do you handle tree-root drain blockages on Maple Row? expand_more
Yes — tree-root intrusion on Maple Row's mature canopy is one of the most common calls we get every spring. We hydro-jet the lateral clear, then run a full HD camera inspection so you can see the state of the pipe. If the clay lateral is cracked or offset, we quote a repair or sleeving solution with a written fixed price before touching anything.
Do you work with Northfield body corporates and property managers? expand_more
We do — including several blocks around Northfield High Street and Pinewood Estates. We provide itemised tax invoices, photo reports, and detailed scope documents that property managers and insurers accept without question. We can also set up scheduled preventative maintenance for communal systems.
Can you supply references from other Northfield customers? expand_more
Yes. If you'd like to speak to another Northfield homeowner before booking — for example someone who's had the same re-pipe or water heater job done — just ask and we'll connect you. We're proud of our local reputation and happy to stand behind it.
