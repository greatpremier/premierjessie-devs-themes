Frequently Asked

Plumbing Questions Answered

Do you really charge zero overtime or weekend rates? expand_more
Yes. A pipe doesn't check the calendar before bursting at 2 AM on Christmas Eve. We provide flat-rate, upfront pricing regardless of the hour, weekend, or holiday.
What qualifies as a plumbing emergency? expand_more
Active burst water lines, main sewer drain backups into tubs or sinks, complete loss of hot water during freezing weather, smell of gas near water heaters, or failing sump pumps during a storm. If water is uncontrollable, call our emergency line immediately.
How does your upfront pricing work? expand_more
Our technician performs a diagnostic assessment upon arrival, explains the issue thoroughly, and provides a fixed dollar figure for the repair. You approve it prior to any work beginning. Even if the repair takes longer than expected, you only pay what you approved.
Are you licensed, bonded, and insured? expand_more
Walt's Plumbing carries full Master Plumber State Licensure (#PL-48920), along with $2,000,000 commercial general liability insurance and workman's compensation on every technician who enters your property.
